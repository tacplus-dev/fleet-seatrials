- **The certificate (#35):** the pipeline owns it. The `cert` job (`ci/cert.sh`) checks the current version of the secret `tls-seatrials` in `tacplus-dev` (`ci/cert-check.sh`: valid, due, expired, wrong-hostname, invalid) and has lego 5.6.0 (pinned by SHA-256 in `ci/tools.sh`) obtain `tacplus.dev` and `*.tacplus.dev` by DNS-01 in the zone `acme-challenge-tacplus-dev` when it is missing, expired, wrong or invalid, or renew it by ARI (else the last third of its lifetime); a new certificate is checked, then added as a new version. The ACME account is kept in `acme-account-seatrials`. A supplied certificate (`CERT_SUPPLIED_SECRET`) is checked and never obtained. `generate`, `diff`, `apply` and `test` install the version `cert` passed on as the job's `crypto.yaml` `pki.edge_tls[0]` (`ci_cert`), and the operator serves it in `CERT_CHAIN` mode: `operator.yaml` has no `tls`, and the render has no letsencrypt workload and no kernel identity for it.
